# HUMAN WORLD — GENESIS ECONOMY SPECIFICATION

## 1. PURPOSE
This document formalizes the Genesis period rules, participation scoring, and token allocation philosophies derived from the v0.3 Economic Constitution.

## 2. THE GENESIS ALLOCATION
The Genesis period represents the earliest verified humans participating in the creation of Human World. The allocation pool for Genesis and Community is currently targeted at 35% of the total hypothetical supply (1,000,000,000 HUMAN).

**Human-Based Eligibility:**
Every eligible verified human (via World ID) may receive an opportunity to earn a Genesis allocation. Allocations are strictly human-based, not wallet-based, to prevent Sybil attacks and automated farming.

## 3. GENESIS SCORE (EARNED, NOT HANDED OUT)
Genesis allocation is determined by a Genesis Score. It is not a flat airdrop.

**Valid Contribution Categories (To Be Calibrated):**
- Verified entry and profile creation
- Active exploration and returning over time
- Creating spaces and building assets
- Attracting genuine visitors
- Organizing events and community contribution
- Constructive feedback during the Alpha/Beta phases

## 4. ANTI-FARMING RULES
Genesis rewards must NOT primarily reward:
- Repetitive clicking
- Meaningless logins
- Artificial activity
- Wallet multiplication
- Automated farming or spam

*World ID establishes human uniqueness, but the Genesis Score algorithm must distinguish human uniqueness from meaningful contribution.*

## 5. EARLY ADVANTAGE VS. NEW-PLAYER MOBILITY
Early entry should provide an advantage (historical status, Genesis allocation, early community recognition), but it must NOT create an unbeatable monopoly.

The ecosystem token reserve (20%) is designed to support future participation after the Genesis period ends. The economic system must allow "Old Capital vs. New Innovation" to compete, ensuring a player entering at 99% population saturation still has upward mobility.

## 6. TOKEN ROLE AND VESTING PHILOSOPHY
**The Role of HUMAN:**
HUMAN is the economic layer that rewards humans for helping Human World exist; it is not the reason the world exists. It may serve ecosystem utility, marketplace functions, and governance, but it should not be required for every basic activity.

**Vesting (To Be Validated):**
- Genesis/Community: Meaningful allocation, potentially locked or streamed to ensure alignment.
- Ecosystem: Multi-year emissions.
- Team/Investors/Treasury: Long-term vesting schedules to be determined by legal and market conditions.

## 7. UNRESOLVED PARAMETERS
- The exact weights of the Genesis Score algorithm.
- The precise token lock/vesting schedule for Genesis participants.
- The exact mathematical division of the 35% pool.
- The cutoff metrics for the end of the Genesis period.
