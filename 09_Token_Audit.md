# HUMAN WORLD — TOKEN AUDIT & DECISION v1.0

## 1. THE PROBLEM
The fundamental question: **What economic problem does a tradable HUM token solve that an internal, non-tradable accounting unit cannot?**

If the answer is "we want to raise money" or "crypto projects need tokens," the economic design fails the No-Ponzi test. WLD already exists as a settlement asset, but external WLD volatility risks destroying the internal game economy if all assets are priced in it.

## 2. ADVERSARIAL COMPARISON

### A. No Token / Fiat-Peg Reference
- **Pros:** Zero regulatory risk. Total price stability for items (e.g., $5 to build a house).
- **Cons:** Breaks immersion. Hard to manage complex micro-transactions and player-to-player economic velocity without a liquid medium of exchange.

### B. Internal Non-Tradable HUM (Off-chain Accounting Unit)
- **Pros:** Perfect control over faucets and sinks. No speculative attacks. Zero regulatory exposure as a security.
- **Cons:** Cannot easily be exchanged for capital to pay real-world bills (limiting the "creator economy"). Capital cannot easily flow in from investors to bootstrap large districts.

### C. Tradable Token Launched Initially
- **Pros:** Instant hype. Immediate founder/treasury liquidity.
- **Cons:** Guaranteed speculative bubble and crash before the game's productive economy can support the token's market cap. 99% probability of economic failure (Axie Infinity model).

### D. WLD-Only Economy
- **Pros:** Leverages the World ecosystem heavily.
- **Cons:** If WLD drops 50% externally, the cost to build a house in Human World doubles in WLD terms, causing a massive deflationary spiral internally. WLD is volatile external capital.

## 3. THE VERDICT

**Recommendation: STAGED DEPLOYMENT (Internal HUM -> Tradable HUM later).**

Phase 1 must launch with an **Internal, Non-Tradable Accounting Unit (HUM)**.
- WLD is used as the on-ramp (buying HUM) and off-ramp (in specific, controlled ways).
- HUM acts as a stable internal gear, absorbing external WLD volatility.
- Faucets and sinks are calibrated in a closed-loop environment.

## 4. THE TOKEN THRESHOLD (CONDITIONS FOR TRADABILITY)

**HUMAN is not the reason Human World exists. HUMAN is the economic layer that rewards humans for helping Human World exist.**

A tradable HUM token will **ONLY** be launched if all of the following objective conditions are met:

1. **Sustained Velocity:** The internal economy must show a minimum of 6 months of sustained HUM velocity (trade between players, not just system faucets).
2. **Sink Dominance:** Total HUM sinks (maintenance, construction, fees) must regularly exceed or match total HUM faucets to prevent hyperinflation.
3. **Productive Utility:** At least 50% of HUM spent must be on productive assets (buildings, services, creator tools) rather than speculative land hoarding.
4. **No Ponzi Dependency:** The system must prove it is not using new player WLD deposits to pay yields to early players.
5. **Sufficient Liquidity Prep:** The treasury must have accumulated sufficient WLD/USDC reserves to seed robust liquidity pools if HUM is bridged on-chain.

**Until these thresholds are crossed, DO NOT LAUNCH A TOKEN.**

## 5. GENESIS VS ECOSYSTEM
- **Genesis:** Rewards early verified humans participating during the founding period. Allocation is human-based, determined by a "Genesis Score" (derived from exploration, building, community contribution), not simply handed out evenly. Early entry provides an advantage, but not an unbeatable monopoly.
- **Ecosystem:** Supports future participation after Genesis, ensuring new-player mobility and preventing the system from becoming a "farming" game that only benefits early adopters. The token should have utility because the world has utility, not the other way around.
