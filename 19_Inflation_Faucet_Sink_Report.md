# HUMAN WORLD — INFLATION & FAUCET/SINK REPORT

## 1. PURPOSE
This report analyzes the internal macroeconomy—specifically the velocity, issuance (faucets), and destruction (sinks) of the internal HUM accounting unit.

## 2. SIMULATED MECHANICS
- **Faucets:** Social activity (5 HUM/step) and Business revenue (10 HUM/step).
- **Sinks:** Building construction (50 HUM) and Land Maintenance Taxes (variable).

## 3. RESULTS (At 5 Million Population)
- **Baseline (Model C):** HUM Velocity (Faucets - Sinks) expanded safely at ~$53M per period. Sinks kept pace with faucets, preventing hyperinflation.
- **+100% Faucet Stress Test:** Doubling the faucet output without adjusting sinks resulted in an immediate accumulation of idle HUM balances. This indicates that if we double rewards, we MUST introduce secondary sinks (e.g., cosmetics, district upgrades).
- **No HUM Sink (WLD Only):** When taxes were removed entirely, the simulation showed rampant hoarding. Inactive players held land indefinitely, choking secondary liquidity.

## 4. CONCLUSION
The closed-loop HUM economy works. Maintenance taxes (sinks) are not optional; they are required to force the recirculation of land from inactive users back into the active economy.
