# HUMAN WORLD — PARAMETER SENSITIVITY REPORT

## 1. PURPOSE
This document tracks how sensitive the Human World economy is to the core Land Supply Ratio parameter.

## 2. RESULTS AT 5 MILLION POPULATION

| Scenario | Land Sold | Treasury (WLD) | Gini | % Zero Land | Top 1% Owns |
|----------|-----------|----------------|------|-------------|-------------|
| Ratio 0.25 | 1.13M | $17.2M | 0.84 | 81.9% | 10.8% |
| Ratio 0.50 | 2.26M | $37.0M | 0.79 | 72.3% | 9.5% |
| Ratio 1.00 | 4.47M | $75.1M | 0.75 | 61.1% | 8.9% |

## 3. ANALYSIS
The expanded simulation (0.1 to 2.0) proves a direct correlation between the Supply Ratio and Economic Inclusion.
- **At 0.25:** The world is too small. Over 80% of players are priced out, acting only as workers or social participants.
- **At 1.00:** The world is spacious. Exclusion drops to 61%. Treasury revenue doubles.
- **At 1.50 and 2.0:** Expanding beyond 1.0 yields diminishing returns on reducing exclusion (drops to ~48% and ~35% respectively) while massively increasing the absolute map size, which risks "ghost town" effects at lower populations.

**Recommendation:** The final parameters should rigidly target a supply ratio of **1.0**. This balances an acceptable Gini (0.75) and zero-land exclusion (~61%) without creating an over-supplied, empty world.
