# HUMAN WORLD — ADVERSARIAL ECONOMIC TEARDOWN v1

Verdict from the research supplied in the project:
CONDITIONAL PASS.

Key findings:
1. A fixed absolute land cap is rejected as a long-term model. A simulation using 2M plots produced severe exclusion and concentration at large populations.
2. Demand/activity/development should influence value, but a mechanism is required; wealth alone cannot be allowed to determine everything.
3. Tokenomics should not be finalized before the economy proves a token is genuinely useful.
4. Per-wallet whale caps alone are insufficient to control concentration; human-level issuance/acquisition rules should be investigated.
5. Land needs use value and productive activity rather than relying on speculation.
6. EVE Online is a useful benchmark for explicit economic faucet/sink management.
7. New-player accessibility is a critical long-term design problem.
8. Human/World ID uniqueness can provide a structural anti-Sybil advantage.
9. WLD volatility means the internal economy should not depend on WLD as a stable unit of account.
10. A future token should not be launched merely to create investor speculation.

Important caution:
The simulation is a directional stress test, not a calibrated forecast. Its assumptions must be reviewed and challenged before using the outputs as predictions.

Core research themes:
- Marx: use value vs exchange value; capital; accumulation; rent.
- Smith: specialization, division of labor, market pricing.
- Hayek: prices as decentralized information.
- Schumpeter: entrepreneurship and creative destruction.
- Keynes: demand and economic cycles.
- Friedman: property, choice and competition.
- Bitcoin: credible and predictable monetary rules, scarcity, network effects.

Potential operating principle:
HUM faucets must be matched against HUM sinks and monitored over time.

Potential productive loop:
Land -> development -> human activity -> demand -> value -> trade -> reinvestment.

Do not treat this document as final truth. Re-test its assumptions in the next simulation.
