Using Economic Constitution v0.2 as source of truth, build HUMAN WORLD ECONOMIC SIMULATOR v2.

Model:
- verified humans
- population growth
- land issuance
- primary purchases
- secondary trading
- buildings
- productive activity
- HUM creation/destruction
- WLD conversion/volatility
- fees
- auctions
- wealth concentration
- inactivity
- new-player entry
- scarcity
- district migration

Run:
100K, 1M, 10M humans
100K -> 1M growth
1M -> 10M growth

Measure:
Gini wealth, Gini land, land access, median wealth, top 1/5/10%, land prices, transaction volume, activity, HUM supply/velocity, faucets, sinks, revenue, new-player outcomes, whale concentration, liquidity.

Stress:
WLD -50%, population +100%, population -30%, demand +300%, whale attack, mass inactivity, speculative bubble, market crash, mass migration, new technology, new district.

Output code, config, raw results, charts and PASS/FAIL analysis.

The goal is to discover failure, not to make the project PASS.
