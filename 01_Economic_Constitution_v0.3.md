# HUMAN WORLD — ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION v0.3

## 1. Core Economic Principles
Human World is a persistent virtual economy. Land has genuine use-value, not just speculative value. The economy is designed to survive scale without acting as a Ponzi scheme.
**HUMAN is not the product.**
Human World is the product. HUMAN exists to allow the humans who build, inhabit and contribute to that world to participate in the ecosystem they help create.

## 2. User Retention Hierarchy
1. I enjoy being here.
2. My friends/community are here.
3. I have built something here.
4. I own things here.
5. My participation may have economic upside if the ecosystem succeeds.
*The system must NOT reverse this order. This is not a pure play-to-earn/farming economy.*

## 3. Product Loop
ENTER → CLAIM / EARN GENESIS PARTICIPATION → EXPLORE → MEET HUMANS → CREATE IDENTITY → BUILD → CONTRIBUTE → EARN REPUTATION + ELIGIBLE REWARDS → OWN SOMETHING → CREATE VALUE → RETURN → GROW

## 4. World ID Role
World ID provides structural anti-Sybil protection, enabling human-level economic limits (e.g., caps on land ownership) that are impossible in standard crypto economies.

## 5. Land System & Issuance
The absolute maximum land supply is not fixed. Supply is dynamic, scaling at a ratio of 0.5 to 1.0 plots per verified human to ensure frontier land is always available.

## 6. Primary Market & Genesis Allocation
Genesis allocation will utilize a bonding curve combined with human-level acquisition caps. This prevents instant buy-outs by early whales.
**Early Entry Advantage:** Early entry provides an advantage (historical status, Genesis allocation), not an unbeatable monopoly.

## 7. Secondary Market & Valuation
The secondary market relies on Offers/Counteroffers and fixed-price listings. AMMs are rejected. Value is discovered through genuine human dwell time and productive activity, separating observable from manipulable metrics.

## 8. Buildings & Businesses
Land must host productive assets. Businesses generate services and goods, acting as the primary driver of internal economic velocity and creating utility for late entrants.
*(Schumpeterian principle: New players must be capable of disrupting old businesses through innovation).*

## 9. New Player Mobility
A player entering after 99% of the original population must still have a viable path to success through building, innovation, creator activity, and ecosystem rewards.
*The economic promise: You are early enough to matter, but not so late that you cannot succeed.*

## 10. Social System
The social layer acts as the engine for price discovery and reputation without converting raw engagement directly into currency.

## 11. HUM (Internal Accounting) & WLD
The economy operates on an Internal, Non-Tradable Accounting Unit (HUM) initially, protecting internal pricing from external crypto volatility (WLD).

## 12. Faucets & Sinks
Faucets (issuance) are strictly tied to productive labor. Sinks (maintenance, construction) must regularly match or exceed faucets to prevent hyperinflation.
**Anti-Farming Principle:** Rewards must NOT primarily reward repetitive clicking or automated farming. They must reward unique-human participation and meaningful contribution.

## 13. Tokenomics Philosophy
The working token allocation is:
- 35% Community / Genesis
- 20% Ecosystem / Player Rewards
- 15% Treasury
- 10% Team / Founder
- 10% Investors
- 5% Liquidity
- 5% Partnerships / Growth
*The humans who create the network should participate meaningfully in the network they help create.*

## 14. Token Vesting & Expectations
Exact vesting schedules (Community, Ecosystem, Team, Investors) are marked "TO BE VALIDATED".
**Warning:** Human World must NEVER promise token appreciation or guaranteed returns.

## 15. Inactivity & Auctions
Unmaintained land reverts to the treasury for public auction to prevent dead capital.

## 16. Token Threshold
A tradable HUM token is strictly undecided. It requires sustained internal velocity (6 months+), sink dominance, and proof of no Ponzi dynamics before launch. The token should have utility because the world has utility, not the other way around.

## 17. Economic Theory Application
- **Marx:** Use value must exist; land gains value through utility/activity.
- **Smith:** Players can specialize (builders, traders, entertainers).
- **Hayek:** Market prices communicate decentralized info.
- **Schumpeter:** New players can disrupt old businesses.
- **Keynes:** Avoid relying entirely on permanent appreciation expectations.
- **Friedman:** Monetary rules must be transparent.
- **Bitcoin:** Scarcity and credible rules, without falsely claiming decentralized network topology.

### REJECTED IDEAS
- Fixed absolute land supply (e.g., 2M cap).
- Pure fixed-price primary sales without caps.
- AMM liquidity pools for unique land plots.
- Immediate launch of a tradable HUM token.
- Raw social engagement (likes/follows) directly printing HUM.
- Reversing the user retention hierarchy to focus primarily on token farming.
